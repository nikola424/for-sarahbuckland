D1: You’re Losing Clients Every Time You Say “It Depends”

Preview Free: the 3 things I’d say instead

Why a vague answer costs you the sale

A woman asks how to work with you, and you say “it depends, let’s jump on a call.”

And to her that sounds like you don’t know what you sell. So she says she’ll think about it, and you never hear from her again.

So here’s what I’d do instead, in 3 simple steps:

Say who it’s for

Say the result

Ask one question

1: Say who it’s for

“It’s for women who already have clients and want to charge more.” Now she knows if it’s her.

2: Say the result

“By the end, you’ll have a premium offer and know how to sell it.” One sentence, no list of features.

3: Ask one question

“Is that where you want to be?” Then let her answer.

Say who it’s for. Say the result. Ask one question.

D6: The Most EFFECTIVE Way To Handle “I Need To Think About It”

Preview Hint: it’s not chasing her

3 ways to answer “I need to think about it”

1. Agree with her. “Of course, it’s a big decision.” Pushing makes her pull away.

2. Ask what’s on her mind. “What part are you thinking about?” Most of the time it’s one thing, and you can answer it.

3. Set the next step. “Shall we speak again on Thursday?” A real date beats “let me know.”
